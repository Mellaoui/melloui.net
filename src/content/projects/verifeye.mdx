## How it works

The vehicle's photos are sent to a vision model, which is asked to list the damage it sees. The answers are short; most of the work, and most of the cost, is the model reading the images.

## Status

Built and running in production at [verifeye.leveluper.com](https://verifeye.leveluper.com).
